Privacy Policy & Security

🔒 DailyInvoicer Privacy Commitment

We believe your business finances and customer records belong solely to you. DailyInvoicer is built with zero advertising trackers, zero analytics telemetry, and strict local-first data isolation.

1. Local-First Data Storage & Isolation

All invoices, client addresses, line item prices, company logos, and tax parameters created within DailyInvoicer are saved directly in your device's sandbox storage (LocalStorage / IndexedDB). Your sensitive business details are never transmitted or sold to third-party ad networks.

2. Encrypted Cloud Backup & Firebase

If you optionally sign in to your Firebase account, your invoice backups are transmitted over end-to-end SSL/TLS 256-bit encrypted channels to your private Google Firebase project (`daily-invoicer`). Access is governed strictly by your authentication credentials and database security rules.

3. On-Device PDF Compilation

High-resolution PDF generation runs entirely in local client memory via HTML5 Canvas. Your company branding, billing totals, and client names are processed locally without passing through external document conversion servers.

4. Secure Payment Deep Links & WhatsApp Intents

UPI QR codes and payment buttons (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Stripe, PayPal) use official direct protocol links (`upi://pay`). Sharing invoices on WhatsApp triggers direct device intent handlers without accessing or storing your personal address book.

5. Complete Data Export & Right to Erasure

You retain 100% control over your data. You can export a full JSON backup of all stored invoices at any time from App Settings or instantly reset your local application storage.

About DailyInvoicer Platform

DailyInvoicer is a high-performance billing and invoice generation suite built for freelancers, small business owners, contractors, and agencies. Designed with a sleek glassmorphic smartphone interface, it provides instant invoice creation, client management, multi-gateway payments, and high-resolution PDF exports.
